Living Structural Interfaces

Dry-laid and historic stone walls along waterways serve as more than physical retainers—they act as refuges for species.

The interstitial spaces and micro-crevices formed by stacked stone provide essential thermal refuge and attachment substrates for macroinvertebrates, mosses, and biofilm communities.
